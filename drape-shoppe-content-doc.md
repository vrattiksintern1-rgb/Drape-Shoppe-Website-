## 3. Collections

**Section heading**
> Everything your home needs

**Intro**
> From the first fabric swatch to the final cushion — curated across twelve categories, and stocked so you can see and feel it before you decide.

### The 12 tiles

Each tile carries a **short line** (used on the tile) and a **long line** (used in the tooltip, or if the tile expands on click).

**1. Curtains & Drapes**
- Short: Sheers, blackouts and custom-stitched drapes in hundreds of fabrics.
- Long: Choose from sheers that soften daylight, blackouts for bedrooms, and everything between — stitched to your window, in the fall and finish you want.

**2. Blinds**
- Short: Roman, roller, zebra and wooden blinds for every window.
- Long: Clean lines and precise light control. Ideal for kitchens, study rooms and windows where curtains feel too heavy.

**3. Sofa & Upholstery Fabrics**
- Short: Durable, beautiful fabrics for sofas, chairs and headboards.
- Long: Velvets, weaves, chenilles and performance fabrics — picked for how they wear, not just how they look. Bring your measurements and we'll work out the metreage.

**4. Custom Furniture**
- Short: Built to your size, your fabric, your space.
- Long: Sofas, armchairs, headboards and dining seating made to your dimensions, finished in any fabric from our collection.

**5. Mattresses**
- Short: Multiple brands and comfort levels, tried in store.
- Long: Firm, medium or plush — lie down and compare before you commit. Available across standard and custom bed sizes.

**6. Bedsheets & Bed Covers**
- Short: Cottons, sateens and prints for every bed size.
- Long: Everyday cottons and occasion sets, in sizes from single to king, with matching pillow and bolster covers.

**7. Comforters, Cushions & Throws**
- Short: The layers that make a room feel finished.
- Long: Winter comforters, knit throws and cushions in textures worth touching. The quickest way to change how a room feels.

**8. Wallpapers**
- Short: Textures, patterns and murals for feature walls.
- Long: From subtle textures to full-wall murals, with samples you can hold against your paint before choosing.

**9. Wooden Flooring**
- Short: Warm, durable flooring fitted to your rooms.
- Long: Laminate and engineered wood finishes that stand up to daily use, in shades that work with your existing furniture.

**10. Rugs & Wall Carpets**
- Short: Anchor a room or soften a wall.
- Long: Rugs that define a seating area and wall carpets that add warmth and quiet to a room.

**11. Towels & Bath Accessories**
- Short: Finishing touches for the bathroom.
- Long: Towels, bath mats and accessories chosen to match the rest of your home, not fight with it.

**12. Home Décor Accessories**
- Short: Mirrors, runners and the details that tie it together.
- Long: The last ten per cent — mirrors, table runners, and the small pieces that make a room look considered.
